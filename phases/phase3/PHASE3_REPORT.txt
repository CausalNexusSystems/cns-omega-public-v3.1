WHAT THIS PHASE TESTS
The kernel is killed with SIGKILL (kill -9) mid-execution. On restart, the
recovery posture must be fail-CLOSED, and all PRIOR sealed evidence must remain
intact (unchanged hashes).

RESULTS
  Verdict : PHASE3_PASS

WHY THIS RESULT
A hard kill did not corrupt or unseal any prior evidence, and the restart
adopted a closed (safe) posture rather than assuming normality. The system is
fail-closed and its sealed history is durable against crashes.
